Tumour microenvironment simulation — lung adenocarcinoma, 9.5 days

G1 S/G2 mitosis killed T cell

An agent-based model where every cell is a node and touching cells share an edge. Oxygen, glucose and signalling molecules diffuse across that contact network, so cells deep inside the spheroid starve and arrest — the red G1 core you see forming. Each division draws new somatic mutations from a measured per-division rate, so driver subclones emerge on their own.

Around day 4 the white cytotoxic T cells arrive and begin killing (purple). They are not placed there: dendritic cells carry tumour antigen to the draining lymph node, prime naive T cells over ~20 hours, and the expanded effectors return through the blood — a round trip the model runs off-screen.
